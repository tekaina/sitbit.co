SitBit Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

SitBit is a habit-building app for adults who spend long hours sitting. It helps users build healthier routines with desk exercises, movement reminders, snack tracking, streaks, quests, and group sports session features.

Delete Your Account & Data

To request deletion of your SitBit account and all associated data:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account.
  2. If you no longer have the app installed, email support@sitbit.co with the subject Delete my SitBit account.
  3. Include the email address used for your account and, if possible, the approximate date you created it.

We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days, unless we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SitBit collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our app and related services. We only collect data needed to provide core app features and improve the user experience.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile details from Google Sign-In or email/password registration.
  • App activity: exercises completed, streaks, quests, ratings, session participation, and related timestamps.
  • Health and fitness information: snack logs, water tracking, movement activity, and habit progress.
  • Location data: approximate and precise location, if enabled, to support nearby games and related app features.
  • Device and diagnostic data: app interaction logs, crash logs, and device identifiers used for app functionality and notifications.
  • Support requests: any information you choose to send us by email.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and personalize SitBit features.
  • Save and sync your progress across devices.
  • Support account creation, login, and email verification.
  • Send notifications, reminders, and account-related emails.
  • Show nearby games and group session features when location is enabled.
  • Respond to support and deletion requests.

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data only with service providers that help us operate SitBit, such as Firebase services used for authentication, storage, messaging, and app analytics.

5. Third-Party Services

SitBit uses Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, and related app services. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

  • Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Terms

6. Location Permissions

If you enable location features, SitBit may collect approximate or precise location to support nearby games and similar functionality. You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time, but some features may stop working.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you request deletion, we remove your data within 30 days unless retention is required by law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including secure transmission and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data using the methods described above.
  • Disable permissions such as location through your device settings.

10. Children's Privacy

SitBit is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and may notify users through the app, email, or other appropriate means.
