SitBit Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

SitBit is a habit-building app for adults who spend long hours sitting. It helps users build healthier routines with desk exercises, movement reminders, snack tracking, streaks, quests, and group sports session features.

Developer: TekainaM  |  Contact: support@sitbit.co

Delete Your Account & Data

You have the right to delete your SitBit account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to delete your data:

  1. Open SitBit and go to Settings → Delete Account.
  2. If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to support@sitbit.co with the subject line: Delete my SitBit account.
  3. Include the email address used for your account and, if possible, the approximate date you created it.

What gets deleted:

  • Your account profile (name, email, avatar)
  • Your exercise history, streaks, quests, and habit logs
  • Your snack and water tracking data
  • Your participation records in group sports sessions
  • Your ratings, rankings, and in-app progress data
  • Any location data associated with your account
  • Any push notification tokens linked to your account

Deletion timeline:

We will permanently delete your account and all associated personal data within 30 days of receiving your request. You will receive an email confirmation when deletion is complete.

What we may retain after deletion:

We may retain certain minimal data beyond the 30-day window only if required by applicable law, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to resolve active legal disputes. Any retained data will be held no longer than legally required and will not be used for any marketing or app features.

Data Retention

We only retain your personal data for as long as it is necessary to provide the SitBit service. The table below describes our specific retention periods by data type:

Exercise history, streaks, and quest logs Until account deletion + 30 days Core habit-tracking functionality
Snack and water tracking logs Until account deletion + 30 days Core health tracking functionality

When the retention period ends or you request deletion, data is securely deleted or anonymized from our systems and our service providers (Firebase / Google Cloud).

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SitBit collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our app and related services. We are committed to being transparent about our data practices and giving you meaningful control over your personal information.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In or email/password registration.
  • App activity: exercises completed, streaks, quests, ratings given and received, session participation, and related timestamps.
  • Health and fitness information: snack logs, water intake tracking, movement activity, and habit progress data you choose to enter.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location, only when you enable it, to support nearby game discovery and related features. We do not store your location persistently.
  • Device and diagnostic data: app interaction logs, crash reports, and device push notification tokens used for app functionality and notifications.
  • Support communications: any information you choose to send us by email when contacting support.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide, operate, and personalize SitBit features and content.
  • Save and sync your progress and settings across devices.
  • Support account creation, sign-in, and security.
  • Send push notifications, reminders, and account-related messages.
  • Show nearby group games and enable Play Together session features when location is enabled.
  • Calculate rankings and leaderboards within the Play Together feature.
  • Respond to account deletion, data access, and support requests.
  • Monitor and improve app stability and performance.

4. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only with the service providers we use to operate SitBit:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, cloud database (Firestore), cloud storage, push notifications (FCM), and crash analytics.

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes beyond what is needed to operate their services.

5. Third-Party Services

SitBit uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Terms of Service

6. Location Permissions

If you enable location features, SitBit uses your device location in real time to show nearby public games. We do not store your precise location persistently in our database. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; if you do, nearby game discovery will not function, but all other app features remain available.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data, including secure HTTPS transmission, Firebase security rules, and access controls. While we take data security seriously, no system is completely immune to unauthorized access, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (see the Data Deletion section above).
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Revoke location or notification permissions at any time through your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@sitbit.co. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

SitBit is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 18, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@sitbit.co.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and may notify users via the app or email. Continued use of SitBit after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
