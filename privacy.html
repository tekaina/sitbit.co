SitBit Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026  |  Effective: May 5, 2026

SitBit is a wellness and fitness app for people who spend long hours sitting. It includes desk exercises, habit tracking, streaks, quests, an AI coach, and a group sports session feature called Play Together. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights over your data.

If you have questions or requests, contact us at sadaaluns@gmail.com.

🗑 Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time using either method below:

  1. In-app: Open SitBit → Settings → Delete Account.
  2. By email: Send a request to sadaaluns@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my SitBit account”. Include the email address linked to your account.

We will permanently delete your account and all personal data within 30 days of receiving your request, unless we are required to retain certain data for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What gets deleted: account credentials, profile data, exercise history, streak records, session participation, ratings, rankings, location history, and any other personally identifiable information stored in your account.

We collect only the data necessary to provide and improve SitBit. The table below summarises what we collect, why, and the legal basis.

Data Type Examples Purpose Legal Basis

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide, maintain, and personalise SitBit features including exercises, streaks, quests, and AI coach guidance.
  • Save and sync your progress securely across devices via cloud storage.
  • Enable Play Together features including session creation, player management, post-game ratings, and skill rankings.
  • Show nearby public games using your location when you have granted permission.
  • Send app notifications, reminders for movement and hydration, and account-related communications.
  • Diagnose and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues.
  • Respond to support, data access, or deletion requests.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third-party commercial purposes, or any form of data brokering.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): We use Firebase for authentication, cloud Firestore database, cloud storage, cloud functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Firebase processes data on our behalf as a service provider. See Firebase Privacy & Security and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other users (in-app): Within Play Together, your display name, avatar, skill score, and session participation may be visible to other app users as part of the social and group-session features.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, property, or safety.

5. Location Data

SitBit requests location permission only to support the nearby games discovery feature within Play Together. Location data is:

  • Collected only when you explicitly grant location permission on your device.
  • Used only to filter and display public group sessions near your current position.
  • Not stored persistently on our servers beyond the active session query.
  • Not shared with third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.

You can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so will disable the nearby games feature, but all other app features remain available.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the service and fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Specifically:

  • Active accounts: Data is retained while your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: Data is permanently deleted within 30 days of a valid deletion request, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Diagnostic and crash logs: Retained for up to 90 days for debugging purposes.
  • Support emails: Retained for up to 2 years for legal and dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase Authentication for secure identity management.
  • Firestore Security Rules to restrict data access to authorised users only.
  • Role-based access controls within the application.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (see Account & Data Deletion above).
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format where applicable.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw location or notification permissions at any time via your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at sadaaluns@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

SitBit is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at sadaaluns@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
