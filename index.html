Frequently asked questions

Who is SitBit for?

SitBit is for adults 18+ who sit for long hours and want gamified movement breaks during the day, an easy way to organize or join local sports sessions after work, and access to verified sports coaches to help them improve.

What is the coaching marketplace?

The SitBit coaching marketplace connects players with verified local sports coaches. You can browse coaches by sport, location, rating, and price — then book a session directly in the app. Available for football, basketball, tennis, padel, volleyball, and running.

How do I become a SitBit coach?

Email coaches@sitbit.co with your sports background and credentials. Our team reviews all applications and verifies coaches before they are listed on the platform.

Is SitBit free?

Yes. SitBit is free to download on Google Play. The iOS App Store version is coming soon. Coaching sessions are paid separately at the coach's listed rate.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. All desk exercises are designed to be done at your desk, in your office chair, or standing at your workstation. No equipment required.

Is SitBit available on iPhone?

The iOS version is currently in review on the Apple App Store. Email us to be notified the moment it goes live.

Can I use the sports organizer without the wellness features?

Yes. Both pillars work independently. Use SitBit purely as a sports organizer, purely as a desk wellness tracker, purely to find a coach — or all three together.

Does SitBit store my data?

Yes. SitBit stores progress and account data needed for streaks, quests, and session history. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account inside the app. If the app is uninstalled, email support@sitbit.co with the subject "Delete my SitBit account".

How do I contact support?

Email: support@sitbit.co — we aim to reply within 48 hours.
