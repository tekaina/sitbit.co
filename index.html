Questions

Straight answers before you start.

What can I do in the SitBit app?+
The Android app combines 40-second hourly desk movement breaks (neck, back, shoulders, wrists, core, and eyes, alongside postureMonk—SitBit’s desk wellness companion), personal progress through XP, levels, badges, daily quests, streaks, and adaptive goals, water and healthy-snack logs (Fuel tracking), group game organizing, nearby match alerts after long desk hours (The Bridge), and coach discovery in the Trainers tab.
Can I organize or join a game without downloading the app?+
Yes for group sports: you can pick a sport, draft match timing and location, and open shared invite links in a browser at app.sitbit.co without installing Android first. Browsing public games and publishing your match require signing in with a free SitBit account. Movement breaks, progress quests, water/snack logs, nearby alerts, and coach discovery are Android app features.
Do I need to be sporty or already have a team?+
Not at all. You can start with 40-second desk breaks on your own in Android, join open recreational matches with team shuffle or waiting lists, or set up a game with friends across 20+ sports.
Which devices and ages are supported?+
The full SitBit mobile app is available on Android via Google Play. Group-game organizing and account sign-in also run in modern desktop and mobile browsers at app.sitbit.co, and platform questions or feedback can be shared through in-app support. SitBit is for adults aged 18+ and is not a medical app—stop any exercise that causes pain or discomfort.
What is free, and what costs money?+
SitBit is free to download on Google Play, and using the app’s core movement, progress, and group-organizing features—as well as the browser organizer at app.sitbit.co—requires no subscription. If you arrange coaching with an independent trainer listed in Android’s Trainers tab, independent trainers set their own session fees separately outside SitBit.
How do coaching and trainer applications work?+
In the Android app, players can browse independent coaches from the Trainers tab; local trainer listings depend on active coaches in your area. Want to coach on SitBit? Create your coach profile, upload the required documents, and submit your application in the SitBit app. For application or upload problems, use in-app support. Please do not email application documents.
How can I get help or manage my account?+
Use in-app support in the SitBit app or on the web first for technical issues, account help, or product feedback. Can’t access in-app support? Email support@sitbit.co. To delete your account in the app, go to Settings → Delete Account (see our Privacy Policy if you no longer have app access).
