Questions

Straight answers before you jump in.

Do I need an account to browse games on the web?+
Yes — browsing open game listings and leaderboards currently requires signing in to a free SitBit account. You can still view individual shared session links, pick a sport, and draft a game before signing in to publish.
Who is SitBit for?+
Adults (18+) balancing long desk hours with recreational sport — whether organizing a weekly game with friends or building regular movement breaks on mobile.
Where is SitBit available?+
Session organizing and sign-in run directly in any modern browser at app.sitbit.co. The full companion app is free to download on Google Play; iOS is coming soon.
How do I become a coach or contact support?+
For coaching applications write to coaches@sitbit.co. For product questions or account help email support@sitbit.co.
