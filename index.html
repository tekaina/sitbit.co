Designed for adults who work at a desk

SitBit is intended for users 18 and older. It is made for office workers, remote workers, developers, students, and professionals who want to break up long sitting sessions with practical movement, better daily habits, and the option to organize sports with friends.

SitBit supports healthy routines, but it is not medical advice. Users should stop any exercise that causes pain and consult a healthcare professional when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Who is SitBit for?

SitBit is for adults 18+ who spend long hours sitting and want structured movement breaks, desk exercises, snack tracking, and habit-building support.

Do I need gym equipment?

No. SitBit focuses on desk-friendly exercises that can be done with little or no equipment.

Does SitBit store my progress?

Yes. SitBit stores progress and account-related data needed to provide features like streaks, quests, and synced history. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How can I delete my account?

You can delete your account inside the app at Settings → Delete Account. If you no longer have the app installed, email support@sitbit.co with the subject "Delete my SitBit account".

How can I contact support?

Email: support@sitbit.co — we aim to reply within 48 hours.
