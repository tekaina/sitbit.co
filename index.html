Frequently asked questions

Who is SitBit for?+
SitBit is for adults 18+ who sit for long hours and want gamified movement breaks, an easy way to organize local sports sessions, and access to verified sports coaches.
What is the coaching marketplace?+
It connects players with verified local sports coaches. Browse by sport, location, rating, and price — then book a session directly in the app.
How do I become a SitBit coach?+
Email coaches@sitbit.co with your sports background and credentials. Our team reviews all applications.
Is SitBit free?+
Yes. SitBit is free to download on Google Play. iOS App Store version is coming soon. Coaching sessions are paid separately at the coach's listed rate.
Do I need any gym equipment?+
No. All desk exercises are designed to be done at your desk, in your office chair, or standing at your workstation.
Is SitBit available on iPhone?+
The iOS version is currently in review on the Apple App Store. Email us to be notified the moment it goes live.
Can I use just the sports organizer?+
Yes. All three pillars work independently. Use SitBit purely as a sports organizer, purely as a desk wellness tracker, purely to find a coach — or all three together.
How do I contact support?+
Email: support@sitbit.co — we aim to reply within 48 hours.
