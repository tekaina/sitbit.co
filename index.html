Frequently asked questions

Who is SitBit for?+
SitBit is for adults 18+ who sit for long hours and want gamified movement breaks, easy local sports organization, and access to verified coaches.
What is the coaching marketplace?+
It connects players with verified local coaches. Browse by sport, location, rating and price, then book directly in the app.
How do I become a SitBit coach?+
Email coaches@sitbit.co with your background and credentials. Every application is reviewed before listing.
Is SitBit free?+
Yes — free to download on Google Play. iOS is coming soon. Coaching sessions are paid separately at each coach's listed rate.
Do I need gym equipment?+
No. All desk exercises work at your chair or standing at your workstation — zero equipment required.
Is SitBit on iPhone?+
iOS is currently in App Store review. Email us to be notified at launch.
Can I use only one feature?+
Absolutely. Wellness, sports organizing and coaching all work independently — use one, two, or all three.
How do I contact support?+
Email support@sitbit.co — we reply within 48 hours.
