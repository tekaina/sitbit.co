A few good questions

Before you get moving.

Do I need to download the app to organize a game?

The focused Play web experience is designed for organizing in your browser. You'll sign in to your SitBit account before publishing or joining. The app adds the wider SitBit experience.

Will I use the same account on web and in the app?

Yes. Use the same sign-in method and account. Your games are part of SitBit, not a separate website-only game system.

Why can't I see games in my area?

Availability depends on games people have published and what can be shown publicly. Try another date or location, or organize your own. An empty list does not mean something is wrong.

Who is SitBit for?

SitBit is designed for adults 18+ who want more movement in their day and a simpler way to bring people together for sports.

Can I explore coaching or become a trainer?

Explore the coaching features in the SitBit app. For questions about joining as a trainer, contact coaches@sitbit.co. Availability and application review apply.

How can I get help?

Email support@sitbit.co. Never share your password or sign-in tokens.
